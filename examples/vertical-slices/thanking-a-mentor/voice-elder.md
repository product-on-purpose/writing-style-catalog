Dana,

For about nine years I thought the reason I got the platform migration in the spring of 2016 was that you had run out of better options. I told you I was not ready, you said I was closer than I thought, and I took that as the kind of thing a manager says when the list of names is short. I believed it for most of a decade, and I was wrong, and I am not going to dress that up as anything other than what it was.

What I remember of those six months is smaller than what I would have guessed. I do not remember the launch. I remember the third week, when I reversed a decision I had made on Monday and reversed it back on Thursday, and you sat through the second reversal without saying anything at all. I remember asking you a question you had already answered, and you answered it again in nearly the same words. I remember that when I handed the work back to you in a panic, you did not take it. That is the whole of what I can report. I cannot tell you what it cost you, because you never let it show on anything I could see, and I was too busy being frightened to look.

Last month I put Priya forward to lead a project she did not feel ready for, and I stayed close, and I did not take it over when she got stuck in the same week of the same kind of work I once got stuck in. It was somewhere in the fourth week that I understood I was doing something I had watched being done, ten years ago, by someone sitting very still across a table. That is how it happened. I did not decide to pass it on. I found that I already knew how.

I do not know whether it will go for her the way it went for me. It went well for me, and it could have gone the other way, and you carried the risk of the other way without mentioning it. I have seen people put forward too early and come apart, so I am not telling anyone to do this. I am telling you what it was like to be on the receiving end of it, from a distance of ten years, which is about how long it took me to see it clearly.

In another ten years the migration will be a line in a story I tell, and the system itself is already half replaced. What I expect will still be standing is the afternoon you did not take the work back.

I wanted you to have that before another year went by. You do not need to answer.

Thank you, Dana.
