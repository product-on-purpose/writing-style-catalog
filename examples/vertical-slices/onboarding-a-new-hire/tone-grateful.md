Mei,

On Monday afternoon you sat beside me while the setup doc failed on the VPN cert step. You did not tell me to read the doc again. You said the doc was wrong, fixed it while I watched, and moved on. Because of that, my first working build came on day one, and I never had the quiet week of feeling blocked and not wanting to interrupt anyone. I had braced for that week. You spared me from it.

On Thursday you let me co-drive the design session for the first change, and you were willing to let a new person say out loud that the plan had a hole. You had already spent hours scoping that task before I arrived, so the hole was a small embarrassment for you. I raised the edge case anyway, you wrote it down, and the room treated it as a contribution. I walked out of that session feeling like a member of the team, and that feeling came from you.

Arjun, you are giving up a real share of your own sprint to pair with me next week, and I know the cost of that. Sprint planning had to bend for it. Because you agreed to review my pull request, I can aim to open it by Wednesday and not wait for a perfect moment. I would not have attempted a first change this early without you standing behind it.

Thank you also to the three people who started threads with me this week about things nobody asked them to cover. I now know who to ask, and not only where to look.

I did not earn any of this in my first five days. It was given to me, and I wanted you to know what it turned into.

Priya
Friday, June 26
