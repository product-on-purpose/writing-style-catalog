Insights is not shipping in Q3. The billing-system migration took the engineering capacity the dashboard was built on, and the dashboard would have gone out missing saved views and scheduled reports. Those are the two things our four key accounts asked for by name. It moves to Q1 2027, with a target of March 13.

I want to say what made that choice a clean one, because it was not mine to make alone.

Dario, you looked at the half-built dashboard and told me plainly that it should not go out. You said it in the planning meeting, in front of people who had been promised a date, and it cost you the easy answer of saying it would probably be fine. That is the reason we are choosing a delay over a release that would have failed at the exact use cases we sold. Because you also scoped the CSV export in two weeks, customers will have their data on September 26 instead of having nothing.

Jordan, you took the four key accounts one at a time. You did not send a notice and wait. You called the ones who leaned hardest on the Q3 date before the written word reached them, and you absorbed the first reaction of each. By the time this message arrives, those customers have already heard it from a person. I did not see those calls, and I owe you for every one of them.

To the billing team: the migration is on track for production the week of September 19 because you worked through the compliance surprises when they surfaced. It is the reason the dates above are dates we can stand behind.

Thank you. I mean each of those specifically, and I will leave it there.
