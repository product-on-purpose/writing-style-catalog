You need to see your usage data without waiting on us, and right now that means waiting on an Insights dashboard that was supposed to ship this quarter. It did not. The billing-system migration we were contractually required to finish ran six weeks past its own deadline, and finishing it on time left no room to also finish Insights to the standard we told you it would meet. We are not shipping a half-built dashboard and calling it done, and we are not asking you to wait for an explanation before you get your data.

Here is what is real. The full Insights dashboard, with the saved views and scheduled reports you were told about, moves to Q1. We are not shipping a stripped-down version early and asking you to call it the same thing.

What you can use today is a CSV export of the same data Insights will eventually surface. From Settings > Data and Analytics > Export, you can download every usage event on your account - user ID, event name, timestamp, session ID, plan tier - from your account's creation date through the end of yesterday. Most exports generate in a few seconds. Open the file in Excel, Sheets, or whatever BI tool your team already runs, and you can build the filtering, grouping, and retention views you need, starting now instead of in Q1.

That is not the dashboard. It takes your own spreadsheet work to get answers the dashboard would have handed you automatically, and it has no scheduled reports and no saved views. If your team was counting on either of those two features specifically, the export will not replace them before Q1.

If you were one of the accounts given a Q3 date directly, expect a call this week, not just this note. If the dashboard's original timeline was part of a renewal decision on your side, tell us now, before that conversation happens, not after. No setup stands between you and the file; it is already in your account settings.

Download your export today, from Settings > Data and Analytics > Export.
