Insights will not ship this quarter. The dashboard moves to Q1, with a target of March 13, 2027, and a CSV export of the same data ships on September 26 so that nobody is left with nothing in the meantime. I am writing to the four accounts that were given the Q3 date, and to the people in sales who gave it to them, and I want to tell you how this happened before I tell you what comes next.

The billing migration ran long. It was not optional, because the contracts and the regulators behind it do not move, and it took the engineers who would have finished the dashboard. I could have shipped Insights on the original date. It would have opened, and it would have had no saved views and no scheduled reports, which are the two things you were promised and the two things you would have opened it to find. I know that outcome well. For about fifteen years I believed a late thing was worse than a thin thing, and I shipped thin things to keep a date. Most of them I had to apologize for twice, once for the thinness and once for the date that had been kept at your expense. I am not saying that will happen to you. I am saying it is what happened to the people I shipped to.

I have broken a date before, and I have been on the receiving end of one. In both seats, what stayed with people was never the month the thing arrived. It was whether the person who had promised it came and said so before they had to ask. That is the only reason this note goes out now, and not after the quarter closes.

I do not know whether March 13 will hold. The Q1 scope is agreed with engineering, but the capacity plan is not closed until Q4 planning finishes, and I have learned not to promise past what I can see. If a second mandatory piece of work appears, I will tell you the same way I am telling you now.

I also do not know whether the CSV will be enough. For some of you it will be. For some of you it will feel like a receipt for a meal you did not get, and I understand that, and I will not argue you out of it. Jordan Park is calling the accounts that asked for a call, the written notice goes to all four, and Dario Reyes has the export on track for September 26.

In a year, this will be a sentence in the history of Insights. This week it is a broken promise, and it is that size to you, and it should be.

Maya Chen
Product Lead
