**To the engineers in India, before the async standup trial starts the week of May 19**

For the past year, you joined our 9am Pacific standup at 9:30pm your time. You did it after a full working day, often from a kitchen table, and you did it without once making it the team's problem. Nobody asked you to keep that up, and nobody on the Pacific side of the call ever paid what it cost.

I want to say plainly what that bought us. Because you kept showing up, a team that grew from 6 to 11 engineers across four timezones still shared one daily picture of the work. When a dependency was about to collide with another one, one of you was usually the person who said so, late in your evening, in a call that was a convenience for the rest of us. We only began to count the cost when the attendance numbers showed it: you averaged 3.2 standups a week, and the missing 1.8 were nights you had already given up enough of.

The proposal to move to async-first standups exists because of you. You never complained, so I had to work out from the attendance figures what the meeting schedule was doing to your evenings. I should have seen it a year ago, and I am grateful you kept going until the numbers made it plain.

Starting the week of May 19, you will post by 10am your own time, and nobody will wait on you at 9:30pm again. The Thursday working session is set for 8am Pacific, which is 8:30pm for you. I know that is still late, and I would rather you tell me honestly whether it is workable than quietly absorb it.

Thank you for the nights.
