To the Halyard team, after the June 13 cutover:

Dani, you called the hold on the March launch while the schedule pressure was real and the February bug was not fully resolved. It cost us three weeks, and you made the call anyway. Because of it, the multi-item orders that would have been corrupted under split payment never reached a customer, and I did not have to learn what that would have looked like.

Marcus, you could have marked the February cart-state bug low severity and moved on. You filed it properly and then fixed it yourself, with nobody asking you to. I only know about it because you wrote it down. The project is alive because you did.

Jordan, you rewrote the payment callback handler over a weekend in April, when we were already behind and a smaller patch was sitting right there. A patch would have shipped. The rewrite is the reason the first peak weekend passed without a single page, and I spent that weekend watching a dashboard that stayed quiet because of what you did on a weekend you could have spent elsewhere.

Sam, on June 9 you held the regression bar when every hour of delay felt enormous and the pressure to ship was at its highest. You did not bend. Because you did not, I could say "no rollback required" on the thirteenth and have it be true.

Fourteen months is a long time to keep two checkout systems healthy at once, and the rest of you carried that quietly: the on-call rotations, the shadow testing, the careful reading of data that no automated suite was going to read for us. None of it will appear in a commit count. I noticed it, and I am saying so here, where you can find it.

Thank you. I am in your debt for this, and I would rather leave it that way than pretend we are even.

Priya
