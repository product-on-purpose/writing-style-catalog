Your customer feedback already exists. It is just not anywhere you can use it. Some of it sits in a spreadsheet someone built months ago. Some of it is buried in a chat thread from last week's sales call. Some of it is a half-finished list in your ticket tracker that nobody has opened since the last roadmap meeting. Before your team can agree on what to build next, someone has to open all three, copy out what matters, and sit through a meeting where everyone argues about priority anyway.

You could try the roadmap view your ticket tracker already has. It will show you tickets. It will not show you that the same request showed up twice in a chat thread and once on a sales call, which is the part that actually tells you what matters.

Tidemark skips the copying. It connects to the feedback sources you already use, finds the themes customers raise more than once across all of them, and ranks those themes by how often and how recently they came up. What comes out the other side is one ranked roadmap, not a spreadsheet you have to assemble and maintain by hand. You see what customers are actually asking for in minutes, not days.

This is built for a specific team: two to twenty people, one person who owns the roadmap, no dedicated research function sitting behind it. If that describes your team, the manual version is costing you a meeting every cycle that Tidemark can skip entirely.

It does not connect to every feedback source yet. The core workflow - gather, rank, share - works today; the list of connected sources is still growing. If the place your feedback lives is not on that list, that is a real gap, not a detail we are hiding, and telling us moves it up the queue.

Once you run it, you get a link. Send it to whoever needs to see the roadmap, and they see the same ranked list you do, not a summary someone wrote about it afterward.
