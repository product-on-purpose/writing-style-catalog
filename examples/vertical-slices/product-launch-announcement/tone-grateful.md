This note goes to the beta teams, not to the launch list. Tidemark opens to the public on June 30. It gathers customer feedback from the chat tool, the ticket tracker, and the survey exports where a team already keeps it, ranks the themes, and produces a read-only roadmap link you can send to stakeholders. Before that happens, we owe something to the twenty-two teams who used it while it was still rough.

You imported feedback you had been tracking in a spreadsheet nobody trusted, and you did it into a product that had no help articles yet. Several of you told us at intake that the spreadsheet was the problem you wanted gone. That sentence shaped what the landing page says and what the first screen of the product shows. You also ran the whole loop, from import to ranked summary to a shared roadmap link, and none of you needed to file a support request. We did not know until you finished that the setup wizard held up without us standing behind it.

Each of you spent working hours on an unfinished tool, and those were hours you could have spent on your own roadmap. You gave them anyway, and the product that opens on June 30 is better for them in ways you will not see, because you will never meet the version it replaced.

To the twenty-two teams: thank you. The launch is yours before it is anyone else's.
